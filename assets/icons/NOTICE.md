# Icons

The SVG icons in this folder are the "New UI" (`expui`) icons of the IntelliJ Platform,
taken unchanged from [JetBrains/intellij-community](https://github.com/JetBrains/intellij-community/tree/master/platform/icons/src/expui)
(`platform/icons/src/expui`).
